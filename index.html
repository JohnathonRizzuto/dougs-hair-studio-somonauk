Introducing

Welcome to Doug's Barbershop.

"Show up for a haircut, stay for the quality company and hospitality."

That's how the regulars put it, and that's the whole idea. Doug's isn't a chain with a wait-list and a screen telling you which stylist is "available." It's one barber who's been doing this a long time.

It's a shop where families have been getting their hair cut for generations — the picky kid who hates the chain places, the guy who knows exactly how he likes his taper, the dad bringing in his boys for the first time.

Doug actually listens to what you want. Classic cuts, a clean straight-razor shave, a beard tidied up right, and the razor-shaped styling he's known for. No fuss, no upsell — just a good haircut and good conversation.
