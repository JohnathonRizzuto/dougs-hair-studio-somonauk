The Shop

Old-school, the way it should be.

"Show up for a haircut, stay for the quality company and hospitality."

That's how the regulars describe it, and that's the whole idea. Doug's isn't a chain with a wait-list and a screen telling you which stylist is "available." It's one barber who's been doing this a long time, in a shop where families have been getting their hair cut for generations.

Doug actually listens to what you want — the picky kid who hates the chain places, the guy who knows exactly how he likes his taper, the dad bringing in his boys for the first time. Classic cuts, a clean straight-razor shave, a beard tidied up right. No fuss, no upsell, just a good haircut and good conversation.

— Doug
